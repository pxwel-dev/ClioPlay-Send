      Settings

      Bluetooth transfer window

      4s

      With ClioPlay v2.2.3+ instant Bluetooth updates, 1–2s is usually enough. Increase this if your head unit takes longer to switch to Bluetooth audio.

      Auto-expand short links

      Automatically resolve shortened map URLs when pasted or shared. Tracking parameters are stripped first.

      Supported links & formats

      • Google Maps Full URLs, maps.app.goo.gl, goo.gl/maps, share.google, g.page, ?cid= / ?ftid=
      • Apple Maps maps.apple.com, maps.apple, maps.apple/p/…
      • Waze waze.com/ul/h… (offline), waze.com/ul/…, Live Map
      • OpenStreetMap osm.org/go/… (offline), pins, map views, directions
      • Coordinates Decimal, DMS (51°30'N), and geo: URIs
